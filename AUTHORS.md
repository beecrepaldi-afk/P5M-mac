# Authors

## P5M for macOS

Created by **beecrepaldi-afk** (https://github.com/beecrepaldi-afk).

Original work in P5M includes, among others: the Takion v20 implementation
(version negotiation, extended header, secp521r1 key exchange, audio FEC
mode 2, trigger effects layout), the Metal renderer and display pacing,
direct VideoToolbox decoding, the CoreAudio output, the controller-driven
interface, the macOS Shortcuts and Siri integration, the session diary
measurements and the problem reports.
